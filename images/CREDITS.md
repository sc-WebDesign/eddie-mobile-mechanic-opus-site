## What was rejected, and why

Twenty-one images were downloaded and looked at across this build and the
original. Rejections worth recording, because the alt text hid every one of them:

- **Two cars nose to nose on jump leads.** Banana plants and monstera in the
  frame. The site is about Edmonton and north London; that photograph is
  visibly a different hemisphere.
- **A red car with the wheel off and sockets on the road.** Perfect composition,
  but the caliper reads **brembo** in white lettering and the car is a modified
  performance saloon. The site makes no claim about parts brands and does not
  do performance work.
- **A toolbox open on the tarmac at golden hour.** A motorbike stands behind it.
  Every job in Eddie's reviews is a car job.
- **A workshop interior with a car on a ramp.** Contradicts the entire premise.
- Supercars, a US-style house at dusk, a meeting room, a driver at the wheel.
