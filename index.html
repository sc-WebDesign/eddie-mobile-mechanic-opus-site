What you are actually buying

He will talk you out of work you do not need.

Trust is the entire transaction with a mechanic, because you cannot see the fault for yourself and you cannot check the repair afterwards. So it is worth setting out plainly how this one works. The car gets looked at properly before a price is given. You are told the cheapest option that will genuinely hold, rather than the most profitable one. And nothing reaches the bill that you did not agree to first.

It works the other way round too. Some faults cannot be put right at the roadside, and when that is the answer you hear it before he starts rather than halfway through. A mechanic willing to turn work down is the one worth keeping the number of.

Questions, answered

FAQs

My car will not start. Can you still come?+

That is the most common callout there is. Batteries and starter motors are two of the jobs he is asked for most, and both are done where the car is stood.

Are you really available at any hour?+

The listing is set to twenty-four hours, seven days. Ring and he will tell you honestly how soon he can be with you rather than leaving you guessing.

Will I know the price before you start?+

Yes. You get the figure before any spanner comes out, and it covers the job you agreed to rather than whatever the job turned into. Where the cheaper repair will do, that is the one you are offered.

Is there anything you cannot do at the roadside?+

Some jobs genuinely need a ramp or specialist kit. When that is the answer you are told up front, rather than having the work started and abandoned, so you are never left worse off than before he arrived.

Do you cover my postcode?+

Enfield, Edmonton and the north London postcodes around them are the core area. Anywhere near the edge of that, ring and ask.
