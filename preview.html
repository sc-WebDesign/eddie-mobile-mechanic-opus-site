FAQs

My car will not start. Can you still come?+

That is the most common callout there is. Batteries and starter motors are two of the jobs he is asked for most, and both are done where the car is stood.

Are you really available at any hour?+

The listing is set to twenty-four hours, seven days. Ring and he will tell you honestly how soon he can be with you rather than leaving you guessing.

Will I know the price before you start?+

Yes. Customers repeatedly mention fair pricing with no unnecessary extras, and one wrote that he looked for the most cost-efficient fix rather than the biggest job.

Is there anything you cannot do at the roadside?+

Some jobs genuinely need a ramp or specialist equipment. When that is the case he says so up front instead of starting work, which is exactly what one reviewer thanked him for.
